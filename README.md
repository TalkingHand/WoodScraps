# Wood Scraps to Planks

Project Zomboid Build 42 mod. Twigs, wood splinters and small wooden items pile up with
nothing to do but burn. This mod breaks them into wood splinters and presses 15 splinters
into a plank with a hammer. No glue.

- **Break into splinters**: twigs by hand (1, a twigs bundle 12). Scrap wood, broken planks
  and wooden items need a knife, axe or saw. Splinters = item weight / 0.2 kg, rounded down,
  at least 1.
- **Press into a plank**: 15 wood splinters + hammer, club hammer or wooden mallet = 1 plank
  (vanilla `Base.Plank`; 15 x 0.2 kg = the plank's 3 kg). Needs Carpentry (Woodwork) 5; gives
  Woodwork XP 5, as for sawing logs. Splitting needs no skill.

44 wooden items are included (forks, spoons, smoking pipes, wooden molds, handles, bats,
hockey sticks, wooden spades and spears, broken wooden items...). Left out: raw materials
(logs, branches, planks, saplings, firewood), items with other parts (nailed weapons,
wooden armor), containers. Medium and small handles give 2 and 1 splinters, because vanilla
carves 6 or 12 of them from one plank.

Recipes only: `media/scripts/WoodScraps_recipes.txt`, generated by `tools/gen_recipes.py`
from the vanilla item weights. `tools/check_loops.py` checks that no vanilla recipe turns a
plank into more than a plank's worth of splinters.

## Install

- Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3835806010) (ID `3835806010`) and enable it in the mod list.
- Manual: copy `Contents/mods/WoodScraps` into `%USERPROFILE%\Zomboid\mods\`.

## Compatibility

No Lua, no vanilla files replaced, vanilla items only: safe to add to or remove from
existing saves. In multiplayer the server needs the mod too.

## Testing

`pzt run` (PZTestHarness): recipes load and are translated, every listed item exists, and 13
crafts through the inventory right-click path, including ones that must fail (14 splinters,
no hammer, no blade, Carpentry 4).
